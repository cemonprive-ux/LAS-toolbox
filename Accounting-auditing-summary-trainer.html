Progression 0 / 11 exercices
Source Document — Read carefully before attempting the exercisesKPMG Newsroom — Adrian King, 4 November 2021

New international sustainability standards board is a 'game-changer'

The International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) Foundation, a not-for-profit international organisation responsible for developing a single set of high-quality global accounting standards, announced the formation of a new International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB) on day three of the COP26. This announcement is a pivotal moment that has the potential to change the way boards and investors make financial investment decisions in the coming years.

Through this new Board, the FRS Foundation aims to put sustainability reporting on the same footing as financial reporting by establishing a sister body to the International Accounting Standards Board (the IASB Board). It is driven by global investors and is being introduced to give sustainability information the same rigor and comparability that the capital markets expect of financial information.

The intention behind the formation of the ISSB is to develop IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards, a comprehensive baseline that provides a common global reporting framework and transparent information for investors and other stakeholders to determine how sustainability impacts an organisations' financial value. The ISSB framework will enable this by changing how actions around sustainability are reported to the market, how climate and broader sustainability issues impact value in the short, medium, and long term, and the way companies view and manage their strategic risks.

The ISSB will sit alongside and work in close cooperation with the IASB, ensuring connectivity and compatibility between IFRS Accounting Standards and the IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards. The ISSB and the IASB will be independent and overseen by the IFRS Foundation Trustees, who are accountable to a Monitoring Board of capital market authorities.

The standards will be developed to ensure compatibility with requirements that are jurisdiction specific as well as standards which are aimed at a wider group of stakeholders. G20 Leaders and the Financial Stability Board have welcomed the IFRS Foundation's work programme to develop global baseline standards for sustainability disclosures.

The FRS Foundation will be taking a "climate first" approach leveraging the Taskforce on Climate related Financial Disclosures (TCFD) Guidelines for its first standard. The TCFD, in its role as a strategic risk framework, has acted as a catalyst to challenge Boards on the way they think about risks, stretched their planning horizons, and ultimately driven better strategic decisions. Taking the structure and thinking of the TCFD as a starting point for the first ISSB standard is a very smart move and will allow them to hit the ground running.

The IRS Foundation Trustees are currently appointing a Chair and Vice-Chair(s) to the ISSB and will commence searching for members to fill the additional board positions.

Given this announcement, you and your organisation should consider whether sustainability is treated as a communications process or as a core strategic risk and opportunity, and whether climate performance is given the attention it needs in this fast-paced and changing environment.

The first IRS Sustainability Disclosure Standard in climate will be a game-changer with the potential to fundamentally change how organisations report their risks and opportunities. Now is the time to prepare.

02

Reformulate & Order

Un résumé reformule sans recopier. Reformulez deux phrases, puis remettez les idées clés dans l'ordre logique d'un résumé.

Reformulate the following sentence in your own words.
"It is driven by global investors and is being introduced to give sustainability information the same rigor and comparability that the capital markets expect of financial information."
Modèle : Global investors are behind this initiative, which aims to make sustainability data as reliable and comparable as financial figures already are.
Reformulate the following sentence in your own words.
"The first IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standard in climate will be a game-changer with the potential to fundamentally change how organisations report their risks and opportunities."
Modèle : This first climate standard could transform the way companies communicate about their risks and opportunities.
Put these key ideas in the logical order of a summary.Utilisez les menus pour numéroter de 1 à 5.
The initiative is backed by the G20 and follows a "climate first" approach based on the TCFD.
The IFRS Foundation announces, at COP26, the creation of the ISSB.
Organisations are urged to prepare now, treating climate as a genuine strategic issue.
The goal is to give sustainability information the same rigour as financial information.
The ISSB will change how firms report climate risk and manage strategic decisions.

03

Summary Conventions

Quelques règles à respecter avant de rédiger. Vérifiez votre compréhension des conventions du résumé.

In a DSCG summary, you should…
Aadd your own personal opinions and develop several detailed examples from your own experience.
Breformulate the author's ideas in your own words, respecting the logical order and word limit.
Ccopy the most important sentences of the text word for word, in their original order.
Dtranslate the entire article into French as faithfully and completely as you possibly can.

Which register is appropriate for a summary?
AA first-person style that openly expresses the writer's own personal opinion throughout.
BA neutral, objective tone written in the third person, with no long direct quotations.
CA series of direct quotations taken from the text and linked together with connectors.
DAn informal, conversational tone, as if you were simply chatting with a close friend.

04

Write Your Summary

Rédigez un résumé du texte en anglais (environ 130–180 mots). Votre texte recevra un retour automatique sur plusieurs dimensions, puis vous pourrez comparer au corrigé.

Corrigé — Résumé modèle

The article discusses the creation of the International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB) by the IFRS Foundation, announced at COP26 in November 2021. The aim is to give sustainability information the same rigour and comparability as financial information, for investors and other stakeholders. The ISSB will change how companies report on sustainability, how climate issues affect value over time, and how firms manage strategic risks. It will work alongside the IASB, under the oversight of the IFRS Foundation Trustees. The initiative already has strong institutional backing from the G20 and the Financial Stability Board, and aims to build a single global baseline standard. Its first standard will follow a "climate first" approach, based on the existing TCFD framework, seen as a smart starting point. As board members are being appointed, organisations are urged to treat sustainability as a genuine strategic risk, not merely a communications exercise. The article presents this first climate standard as a game-changer for corporate reporting. (≈ 155 mots)

structure : sujet → objectif de comparabilité → ce que change l'ISSB → gouvernance → soutien institutionnel → première norme "climate first" → conclusion

Résumé modèle

The article discusses the creation of the International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB) by the IFRS Foundation, announced at COP26 in November 2021. The aim is to give sustainability information the same rigour and comparability as financial information, for investors and other stakeholders. The ISSB will change how companies report on sustainability, how climate issues affect value over time, and how firms manage strategic risks. It will work alongside the IASB, under the oversight of the IFRS Foundation Trustees. The initiative already has strong institutional backing from the G20 and the Financial Stability Board, and aims to build a single global baseline standard. Its first standard will follow a "climate first" approach, based on the existing TCFD framework, seen as a smart starting point. As board members are being appointed, organisations are urged to treat sustainability as a genuine strategic risk, not merely a communications exercise. The article presents this first climate standard as a game-changer for corporate reporting.

Points clés (support oral)

  • Subject — the IFRS Foundation creates the ISSB at COP26 (Nov. 2021).
  • Goal — give sustainability information the same rigour as financial information.
  • What changes — reporting, valuation of climate impact over time, strategic risk management.
  • Governance — ISSB works with the IASB, overseen by the IFRS Foundation Trustees.
  • Backing — strong support from the G20 and the Financial Stability Board.
  • First standard — "climate first" approach, based on the existing TCFD framework.
  • Conclusion — organisations are urged to prepare now; a genuine strategic issue, not just communication.
