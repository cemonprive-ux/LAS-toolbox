Build the Introduction

Une introduction efficace amène le sujet, pose la problématique, puis annonce le plan.

Which sentence is the best opening line (amorce) for this introduction?

AIn November 2021, at COP26, the IFRS Foundation announced the creation of a new board dedicated entirely to sustainability reporting.

Introduction : 0 mot(s)

Modèle — Introduction

For decades, financial reporting has told investors everything about profit, but almost nothing about a company's impact on the climate. In November 2021, at COP26, the IFRS Foundation created the International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB) to close that gap, giving climate information the same rigour and comparability as financial figures. This raises a central question: to what extent can such standards actually help fight climate change? This essay will first show that they are a genuine lever for accountability, then examine their limits, before arguing that they are best understood as a necessary but insufficient piece of the response.

Develop Part I

Thèse · A real lever

Présentez les nouvelles normes comme un véritable levier de transparence et de pilotage du risque climatique. Rédigez un paragraphe argumenté avec une phrase d'amorce, des arguments et des exemples (≈ 120–180 mots).

Partie I : 0 mot(s)

Corrigé — Partie I : un véritable levier

  • Les normes donnent aux investisseurs une information climatique aussi rigoureuse que l'information financière.The ISSB was created to give sustainability information "the same rigor and comparability" as financial information.Source : texte
  • Le climat devient un risque stratégique pour les conseils d'administration, et non un simple exercice de communication.Boards are pushed to treat climate as a core strategic risk rather than a communications exercise.Source : texte

  • Le projet bénéficie d'un fort soutien institutionnel (G20, Financial Stability Board), ce qui lui donne une portée mondiale.Backing from the G20 and the Financial Stability Board gives the initiative real global scale.Source : texte
  • En redirigeant les capitaux, la transparence crée une incitation financière concrète à agir — un mécanisme de marché qui déborde le texte.Better disclosure can redirect capital towards companies that manage climate risk responsibly.Source : connaissances

Develop Part II

Antithèse · An insufficient tool

Montrez que ces normes de reporting, seules, ne suffisent pas à réduire les émissions. Paragraphe argumenté (≈ 120–180 mots).

Partie II : 0 mot(s)

Corrigé — Partie II : un outil insuffisant

  • Une norme de reporting ne réduit, par elle-même, aucune émission — c'est un constat qui déborde le texte.A disclosure standard does not, by itself, cut a single tonne of CO2.Source : connaissances
  • Les normes restent « jurisdiction specific », donc l'adoption reste inégale selon les pays.Standards are developed to fit requirements that are "jurisdiction specific", so adoption remains uneven.Source : texte
  • Sans audit rigoureux ni sanctions, le risque de greenwashing demeure.Without strict auditing or penalties, companies may comply on paper while changing little in practice.Source : connaissances
  • Le texte ne dit rien des obligations contraignantes plus fortes, comme la CSRD européenne — une norme qu'il faut connaître par ailleurs.The article says nothing about binding frameworks such as the European CSRD, which impose real obligations.Source : connaissances
  • L'efficacité dépend surtout de la réaction des investisseurs à l'information publiée, pas d'une obligation de résultat.Its effectiveness depends on investors acting on the disclosed information, not on any obligation of result.Source : connaissances

Transition II → III

Cette transition doit reconnaître les limites de la Partie II tout en ouvrant vers une solution équilibrée (synthèse). Rédigez la vôtre, puis comparez au modèle.

Modèle — Transition II → III

Yet these limits do not mean that accounting standards are useless; they mean that they should be seen as one piece of a larger response, not as a solution on their own.

Develop Part III

Synthèse · A necessary complement

Proposez un équilibre : les normes comme condition nécessaire mais non suffisante, à combiner avec la réglementation contraignante et l'audit. Paragraphe argumenté (≈ 120–180 mots).

Corrigé — Partie III : un complément nécessaire

8

Write the Conclusion

Reprenez le bilan des deux thèses, répondez à la problématique, puis ouvrez sur une perspective plus large. ≈ 80–120 mots.

Conclusion : 0 mot(s)

Corrigé — Conclusion

New environmental accounting standards are a genuine step forward: they give investors and boards climate information as rigorous as financial data, and push companies to treat climate as a strategic risk. However, a reporting standard cannot, by itself, cut emissions; its impact depends on audit quality and on how investors act on the disclosed information. Accounting standards can therefore support the fight against climate change, but they cannot win it alone. They should be seen as one piece of a wider system that also needs binding regulation, rigorous auditing and genuine political will.

Corrigé complet

Le corrigé intégral, structuré en points pour servir de support à une restitution orale. Bouton d'impression en bas.

Introduction

For decades, financial reporting has told investors everything about profit, but almost nothing about a company's impact on the climate. In November 2021, at COP26, the IFRS Foundation created the International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB) to close that gap, giving climate information the same rigour and comparability as financial figures. This raises a central question: to what extent can such standards actually help fight climate change? This essay will first show that they are a genuine lever for accountability, then examine their limits, before arguing that they are best understood as a necessary but insufficient piece of the response.

I. A real lever for accountability

  • Information climatique aussi rigoureuse que l'information financière.The ISSB gives sustainability information "the same rigor and comparability" as financial information.Source : texte
  • Le climat devient un risque stratégique pour les conseils d'administration.Boards are pushed to treat climate as a core strategic risk, not mere communication.Source : texte
  • Approche « climate first » appuyée sur le TCFD.The "climate first" approach builds on the proven TCFD framework.Source : texte
  • Fort soutien institutionnel (G20, Financial Stability Board).Backing from the G20 and the Financial Stability Board gives the project global scale.Source : texte
  • Redirection des capitaux vers une gestion responsable du risque climatique.Better disclosure can redirect capital towards climate-responsible companies.Source : connaissances

Transition I → II — However, giving companies a new reporting standard is not the same as forcing them to cut their emissions — and this is precisely where the limits of the ISSB framework appear.

II. An insufficient tool on its own

  • Une norme de reporting ne réduit aucune émission par elle-même.A disclosure standard does not, by itself, cut a single tonne of CO2.Source : connaissances
  • Adoption inégale selon les juridictions.Standards remain "jurisdiction specific", so adoption is uneven across countries.Source : texte
  • Risque de greenwashing sans audit rigoureux.Without strict auditing, companies may comply on paper while changing little in practice.Source : connaissances
  • Pas d'obligations contraignantes comme la CSRD.The article says nothing about binding frameworks such as the European CSRD.Source : connaissances
  • Efficacité dépendante de la réaction des investisseurs, non d'une obligation de résultat.Effectiveness depends on investors acting on disclosure, not on any obligation of result.Source : connaissances

Transition II → III — Yet these limits do not mean that accounting standards are useless; they mean that they should be seen as one piece of a larger response, not as a solution on their own.

III. A necessary complement

  • Coexistence avec une réglementation contraignante (CSRD, taxonomie verte).Disclosure standards should coexist with binding rules such as the CSRD.Source : connaissances
  • Audit rigoureux et sanctions contre le greenwashing.Rigorous auditing and real penalties are needed to prevent greenwashing.Source : connaissances
  • Gouvernance ISSB/IASB sous tutelle des IFRS Foundation Trustees.Oversight by the IFRS Foundation Trustees gives the framework institutional legitimacy.Source : texte
  • L'information est la condition préalable à l'action, pas un substitut.Disclosure is the precondition for action, but cannot replace action itself.Source : connaissances
  • Cadre mondial (G20) qui facilite la coordination internationale.A globally-backed baseline helps coordinate the international response a global problem requires.Source : texte

Conclusion

New environmental accounting standards are a genuine step forward: they give investors and boards climate information as rigorous as financial data, and push companies to treat climate as a strategic risk. However, a reporting standard cannot, by itself, cut emissions; its impact depends on audit quality and on how investors act on the disclosed information. Accounting standards can therefore support the fight against climate change, but they cannot win it alone. They should be seen as one piece of a wider system that also needs binding regulation, rigorous auditing and genuine political will.
